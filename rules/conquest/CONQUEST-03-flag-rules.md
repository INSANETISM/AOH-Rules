# Flag Rules

Faction flags are used to determine Conquest rewards.

---

## Flag Placement

Faction flags must be raised before raid weekend begins.

If you have a registered faction, you must have your faction flag raised.

---

## Registered Flags

Only the faction's registered flag may be submitted to the trader for Conquest points.

---

## Repeat Flag Restrictions

The same faction's flag may not be turned in on consecutive raid weekends.

---

## Submission Deadline

Captured faction flags must be turned into the trader by:

**The first server reset after raid weekend has ended.**

---

## Flag Capture Evidence

To claim Conquest points for an enemy faction flag:

Players must provide a clip showing:

- The removal of the enemy faction flag from their base.
- The player in possession of the captured flag.
- The flag being delivered to the trader.

No points will be awarded without valid evidence.

---

## Trader Unavailable

If a trader is unavailable:

Players must:

- Be present at trader.
- Open a ticket with staff.
- Provide clips showing the flag in their possession.

This must be completed before the submission deadline.
