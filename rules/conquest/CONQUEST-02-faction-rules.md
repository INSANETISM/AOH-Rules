# Faction Rules

Faction membership and participation are controlled during Conquest events.

---

## Faction Lock

Faction sizes are locked every:

**Wednesday at 9:00PM EST** before raid weekend begins.

---

## Membership Restrictions

After the faction lock:

- Members cannot be added.
- Members cannot be removed.

The faction roster remains locked until raid weekend has ended.

---

## Raid & Defence Rules

Players may only raid and defend with members of their own registered faction.

---

## Outside Assistance

### Raiding

Raiding with players outside your registered faction will result in all flag rewards being forfeited.

### Defending

Defending with players outside your registered faction will result in fines.

---

## Eligible Targets

A faction may only raid factions up to:

**4 members smaller than their own faction** for Conquest rewards.

If a faction attacks a group outside this requirement, their captured flag will have no value.
