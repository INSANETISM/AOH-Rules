# General

The following rules apply to all Conquest activities and point submissions.

---

## Team Kills

Team kills do not count towards any Conquest points.

This includes:

- Faction members.
- Group members.
- Friends intentionally assisting each other.

---

## PvP Tickets

All PvP tickets must be submitted within:

**24 hours of the kill.**

Tickets submitted after this timeframe may not be eligible for points.

---

## Killstreak Rewards

Killstreak rewards are only provided after you have died on your killstreak account.

Please shown evidence of your death and killstreak.

---

## Player Responsibility

Players are responsible for:

- Taking and providing screenshots.
- Submitting tickets for points.
