# Fair Play

Conquest is intended to be a competitive event based on fair gameplay.

---

## Rule Violations

Any rule violations committed while attempting to earn Conquest points may result in:

- Point deductions.
- Fines.
- Disqualification.
- Additional server punishment depending on the severity of the offense.
