# No Build Zones

Players may not build bases in restricted areas.

---

## Restricted Areas

Building is prohibited in:

- Airfields
- Military Zones
- Within 1000m of a bunker
- Gaz Zones
- Main Roads
- Kopa Prison

---

## Bunker Restriction

The 1000m restriction includes:

- Bases
- Stashes
- Storage locations
- Any other player placed structures

---

## Enforcement

Structures placed inside restricted areas may be removed by staff.
