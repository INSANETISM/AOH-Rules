# Log Walls

Excessive log wall placement is not allowed.

---

## Restrictions

Players may not use excessive log walls to create unreasonable barriers or negatively impact gameplay.

---

## Allowed Use

Blocking a standard doorway is acceptable.

---

## Enforcement

Staff may review excessive log wall placement and require adjustments if it impacts fair gameplay.
