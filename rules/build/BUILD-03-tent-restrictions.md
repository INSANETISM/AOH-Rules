# Tent Restrictions

Tents are storage only.

Players may not use tents as walls, barriers, or raid protection.

---

## Prohibited Uses

Players may not:

- Stack tents inside each other.
- Use tents as walls.
- Use tents as barriers.
- Use tents as raid protection.
- Block entrances, doors, gates, vehicle access, or raid paths with tents.

---

## Entrance Definition

An entrance is defined as:

Any opening that allows a player **with a backpack equipped** or a vehicle to enter a base.

---

## Enforcement

Tents used in violation of these rules may be removed and may result in staff action.
