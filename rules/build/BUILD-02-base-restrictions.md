# Base Restrictions

Players and factions must follow the restrictions below when creating bases.

---

## Main Base Limit

Players are limited to:

- 1 main base per faction, group, or solo player.
- 1 custom per faction, group or solo player.

---

## Radar Restrictions

Each faction is limited to:

- 1 radar total.

---

## Base Distance

Bases must be:

**400m apart**

---

## Gate Limit

A maximum of:

**30 gates**

are allowed within your 200m radar area.

---

## Watchtower Height

If placing a faction flag on a watchtower:

- The watchtower may only be a maximum of **10 floors high**.

---

## Enforcement

Bases that exceed these limits may be subject to staff review and required adjustments.
