# Team Kills

Bounties must be claimed legitimately.

Players may not work together to bypass or exploit the bounty system.

---

## Prohibited Actions

The following are not permitted:

- Claiming a bounty placed on yourself.
- Allowing members of your faction, group, or friends to claim your bounty.
- Arranging with another player to remove your bounty for personal benefit.
- Intentionally feeding bounty rewards to another player or faction.

---

## Fair Play

Bounties are intended to create genuine player interactions.

Any attempt to manipulate or abuse the bounty system for financial gain or to avoid normal gameplay is prohibited.

---

## Enforcement

Evidence of arranged bounty claims or bounty exploitation may void the bounty money claimed and a replacement bounty being added.
