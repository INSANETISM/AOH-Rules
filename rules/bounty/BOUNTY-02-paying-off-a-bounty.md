# Paying Off a Bounty

An active bounty may be paid off before it expires.

---

## Early Removal

A bounty may be paid off by:

- The bounty target.
- Any other player on behalf of the bounty target.

Once the bounty has been paid, it is immediately removed and can no longer be claimed.

---

## Important

Paying off a bounty early ends the bounty regardless of how much time remains on the 6-hour timer.
