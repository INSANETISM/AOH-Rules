# Safezone Use

Players with an active bounty may not use the Radio Zenit safe zone to wait out their bounty timer.

---

## Restrictions

Players with an active bounty may not:

- Remain in the safe zone to avoid bounty hunters.
- Use the safe zone to intentionally wait for the 6-hour bounty timer to expire.
- Abuse the safe zone to avoid the risks associated with having an active bounty.

---

## Trading Exception

If a player receives a bounty while already inside the trader safe zone, they may remain a reasonable amount of time to complete their current trade.

Once the trade has concluded, the player must leave the safe zone.

---

## Alternate Accounts

Players may use an account **without an active bounty** to access the safe zone, provided all other server rules are followed.

---

## Related Rules

Having an active bounty does **not** override Safezone rules.

Bounty hunters may not engage or kill bounty targets within the safe zone.

See:

**TRADER-02 No Kill Zone**
