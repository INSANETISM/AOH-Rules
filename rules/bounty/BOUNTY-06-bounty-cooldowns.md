# Bounty Cooldowns

A bounty cannot be repeatedly placed on the same player without a cooldown.

---

## Cooldown Period

Once a bounty has ended, players must wait:

**24 hours**

before placing another bounty on the same gamertag.

---

## When Does the Cooldown Start?

The 24-hour cooldown begins when the bounty:

- Is claimed.
- Expires naturally.
- Is paid off early.

---

## Enforcement

Attempting to bypass the cooldown may result in the bounty being removed and further staff action.
