# Dismantling

Limited dismantling is allowed during raid weekends.

---

## Restrictions

Dismantling may only be performed when necessary to complete a raid.

Players may not dismantle structures unnecessarily or use dismantling to grief another player's base.

---

## Evidence Required

Players must record clips of anything they dismantle during a raid.

Evidence may be requested by staff during rule reviews.
