# Raid Weekend

During official raid times, players may use the approved raid methods listed below.

---

# Allowed Raid Methods

- Player stacking
- Backpack jumping
- Boosting off vehicles
- Using in-game structures to vault or jump
- Code raiding
- Explosives

---

# Forbidden Raid Methods

During raid times, players may not build into another player's base.

Players may also not use:

- Fence kits
- Watchtower kits
- Flag pole kits
- Tents
- Garden plots
- Fireplaces
- Any similar exploit to raid a base.
