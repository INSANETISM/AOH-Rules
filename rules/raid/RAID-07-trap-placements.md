# Trap Placements

Trap placement rules depend on whether raid weekend is active.

---

## During Raid Times

During raid times, traps may be placed inside enemy bases.

---

## Outside Raid Times

Outside of raid times:

- Traps may not be placed within **200m** of another player's main base.

---

## Stashes

Stashes are not protected under this rule.

Players may place traps around stashes at any time.
