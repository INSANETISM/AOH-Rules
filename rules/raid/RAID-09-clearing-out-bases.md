# Clearing Out Bases

Raiders may take a reasonable amount of storage and loot from a raided base.

---

## Restrictions

Players may not completely strip a base of all resources.

The purpose of raiding is not to remove every item from another player's ability to continue playing.

---

## Staff Review

What is considered excessive will be judged by admins on an individual base-by-base basis.

Staff decisions will consider:

- The size of the base.
- Amount of loot removed.
- The amount of loot owned by the raiders.
- Available evidence.
