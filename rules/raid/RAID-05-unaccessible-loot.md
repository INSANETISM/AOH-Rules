# Unaccessible Loot

Players may not hide loot in inaccessible areas of their base.

Loot must be stored in areas that can reasonably be accessed during normal gameplay and raiding.

---

## Examples

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Storage placed at the top of watchtowers with no stairs.
- Loot hidden inside custom base walls.
- Any location intentionally designed to prevent access to stored items.

---

## Enforcement

Staff will determine whether a location is considered inaccessible based on the circumstances and available evidence.
