# Raid Times

Raid weekends occur **biweekly**.

---

## Raid Schedule

**Friday 7:00PM EST → Sunday 7:00PM EST**

Once **7:00PM EST Sunday** is reached, raid weekend has ended.

Any players inside another player's base must leave immediately.

---

## Raid Weekend Restrictions

During raid weekends:

- Container damage is disabled.
- Players must follow all approved raid methods.
- Any captured faction flags must follow conquest point requirements.

---

## Enemy Flag Capture

To receive points for capturing an enemy faction flag:

- Record a clip showing the removal of the flag from an enemy base.
- Turn the flag into trader.
- Submit the clip to staff in a conquest ticket for verification and points.

---

## Conquest Points

A faction may only receive conquest points for captured faction flags **every other raid weekend**.

Each faction flag capture is subject to conquest rules.
