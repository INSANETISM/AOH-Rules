# Non Raid Weekend Base Entry

During non-raid times, players may enter another player's base under specific conditions.

---

## Allowed Entry Methods

Players may enter a base by:

- Walking through an unlocked entrance.
- Using legitimate in-game parkour.

---

## Parkour Rules

Parkour may only be performed using:

- Buildings
- Walls
- Rails
- Other existing in-game structures

Players may not use:

- Vehicles
- Other players
- Backpacks
- Any external method to gain access.

---

## Leaving The Base

If you enter a base using parkour or by walking in:

- You must leave using the same method.
- You may not dismantle structures to exit.
- Vehicles may be used to exit only if they are available through normal access.

---

## If You Become Trapped

If you cannot walk or parkour back out of the base, you must die.

Players may not use dismantling or other methods to escape.

---

## Evidence Required

Players must have clips showing both entry and exit during non-raid times.

Failure to provide evidence may result in automatic punishment.

---

## Additional Restriction

Spinning of locks is not permitted during non-raid times.
