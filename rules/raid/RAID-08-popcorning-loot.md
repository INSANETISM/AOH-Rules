# Popcorning Loot

"Popcorning" is strictly forbidden.

Players may not intentionally ruin, dump, scatter, hide, or despawn another player's loot or storage with the purpose of griefing another group or player.

---

## Examples

This includes but is not limited to:

- Removing loot from storage and intentionally scattering it.
- Hiding another player's items to prevent recovery.
- Destroying or despawning storage items purely to grief.

---

## Vehicles

Shooting out another player's vehicles is not protected under griefing rules.

Players should ensure vehicles are cleared of valuables.
