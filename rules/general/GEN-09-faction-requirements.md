# Faction Requirements

Players must meet the requirements below to create and operate a faction.

---

## Minimum Members

The minimum amount of members required to create a faction is:

**4 players**

---

## Maximum Members

The maximum faction size is:

**12 players**

---

## Duo / Trio Options

Players with fewer than 4 members may use the available duo/trio options.
