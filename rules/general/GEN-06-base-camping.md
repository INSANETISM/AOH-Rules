# Base Camping

Players may not camp a base for excessive periods.

---

## What Is Considered Base Camping?

Repeatedly remaining in the area of another player's base for extended periods with the intent to prevent normal gameplay may be considered excessive base camping.

---

## Reporting

If you believe your base is being excessively camped, open a rule-violations ticket for admin review.
