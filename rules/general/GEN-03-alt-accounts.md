# Alt Accounts

Players are allowed to use additional accounts within the following limits.

---

## Account Limits

Players are allowed:

- 1 Main Account
- 2 Alternate Accounts

---

## Exceptions

Exceptions may be made for approved staff accounts.

Any account outside of these limits may be subject to review and action.
