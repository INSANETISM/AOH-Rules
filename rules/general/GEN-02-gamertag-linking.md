# Gamertag Linking Required

All players must link their gamertag before playing on the server.

---

## Linking Your Account

Players must link their gamertag through:

🔗・gamertag-link

---

## Time Limit

Players have **1.5 hours** to link their account.

Failure to link your gamertag within this timeframe will result in an automatic ban.

---

## Discord Access

Players must link their gamertag to receive full access to the Ashes of Humanity Discord.
