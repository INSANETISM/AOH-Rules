# Faction Rules

Players who register as a faction must follow the requirements below.

---

## Faction Identification

During raid weekends:

- Your faction flag must be flown.
- Players must only wear armbands belonging to their registered faction.

---

## Conquest Participation

If you register as a faction, you are automatically entered into the conquest.

---

## Enforcement

Failure to follow faction requirements may result in staff review and disciplinary action.
