# Requesting a Trader

Players can request a trader through trader chat.

---

## How To Request A Trader

Please ping:

**@Trader**

in:

🛒・trader-chat

---

## Waiting For A Trader

Please be patient while waiting for a trader.

Traders are real people and may need time before switching over to assist.

---

## Respect

Do not repeatedly ping or pressure traders while waiting for assistance.
