# Safezone Respect

Players are expected to respect the trader area and other players using the safe zone.

---

## Trader Area Behaviour

Players should:

- Avoid excessive noise near the trader window while players are trading.
- Treat traders and other players with respect.
- Keep the area clean.

---

## Waste Disposal

Fire barrels are provided for burning unwanted items.

Players should use these instead of leaving unwanted items around the trader area.
