# Restricted Area

The inside of the trader building is restricted for players.

---

## Building Access

Players may not enter the restricted trader area.

---

## Enforcement

Entering the restricted building area will trigger an automatic ban through the server system.

Players should remain outside the restricted area and interact with traders through the approved trading area.
