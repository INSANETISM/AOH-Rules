# Restricted Actions

The following actions are not permitted within the Radio Zenit safe zone area.

---

## PvP Restrictions

Players may not:

- Camp the safe zone to kill players entering or leaving.
- Engage in PvP when the surrounding water towers are fully visible.
- Point weapons at other players.
- Punch other players.

---

## Building & Storage Restrictions

Players may not:

- Build inside the safe zone.
- Store or stash loot inside the safe zone.
- Place tents.
- Place fireplaces.
- Place garden plots.

---

## Vehicle Restrictions

Players may not:

- Enter other players' vehicles.
- Interact with other players' vehicles without permission.

---

## Loot Restrictions

Players may not:

- Take other players' loot.
- Move, interfere with, or mess with another player's belongings.

---

## Enforcement

Violations of safe zone restrictions may result in staff action.
