# Taxi Usage

Community taxis are available for all players to use.

---

## Taxi Restrictions

Players must not:

- Remove parts from community taxis.
- Damage or intentionally interfere with taxi vehicles.

---

## Taxi Despawn

Taxis may despawn due to:

- Server reset.
- Being outside the required proximity distance of more than 5m.

---

## Lost Inventory

Use taxis at your own risk.

If a taxi despawns, any inventory stored inside the vehicle will be lost.

Players are responsible for their own items when using community taxis.
